# **Propositional Variables**

## Main Idea
Propositional variables are variables used to represent propositions, for example it is often that textbooks uses *p* , *q* ,*r* ,*s*  as proposition variable.

My example:<br>
*p*: "It is raining."<br> *q*: "I bought a laptop"<br> *r*: "I wear glasses"<br> *s*: "Python is widely used in Machine Learning"

Sources:<br>Discrete Mathematics and Its Application, Kenneth Rosen (1.1.2 Proposition)<br>
