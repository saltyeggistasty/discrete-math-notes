# **Inverse**

## Main Idea
Let *p* and *q* be propositions, then ¬*p* → ¬*q* is the inverse of the conditional statement *p* → *q*


My example:
  - Let *p*: "Chickens can fly to mars" and *q*: "Cats can drive bicycle", *p* → *q* = If chickens can fly to Mars, then cats can drive bicycles. The inverse then would be ¬*p* → ¬*q* = If chickens cant fly to mars then cats cant drive bicycle.
  

Source:<br>
