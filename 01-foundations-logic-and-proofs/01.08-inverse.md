# **Inverse**

## Main Idea
Let *p* and *q* be propositions, then ¬*p* → ¬*q* is the inverse of the conditional statement *p* → *q*


My example:
  - Let *p*: "Chickens can fly to mars" and *q*: "Cats can drive bicycle", *p* → *q*= If chickens can fly to Mars, then cats can drive bicycles. The inverse then would be ¬*p* → ¬*q* = "If chickens can't fly to mars then cats can't drive bicycle."<br>
  - *p*: "Linux is used worldwide" and *q*: "Binary number system is the foundation of modern computing", then ¬*p* → ¬*q* = "If linux isn't used worldwide then binary number system isn't the foundation of modern computing"
