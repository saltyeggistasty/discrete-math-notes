# **Converse**

## Main Idea
Let *p* and *q* be propositions, then *q* → *p* is the converse of the conditional statement *p* → *q*


My example:
  - Let *p*: "Early computers were large in physical size." *q*: "Discrete math is important in understanding Computer Science", then *q* → *p*: "If Discrete math is important in understanding computer science then early computers were large in physical size".
