# **Converse**

## Main Idea
Let *p* and *q* be propositions, then *q* → *p* is the converse of the conditional statement *p* → *q*

| *p* | *q* | *q* → *p* |
| - | - | ----- |
| T | T | T     |
| T | F | T     |
| F | T | F     |
| F | F | T     |


My example:
  - Let *p*: "Early computers were large in physical size." *q*: "Discrete math is important in understanding Computer Science", then the converse *q* → *p*: "If Discrete math is important in understanding computer science then early computers were large in physical size".
  - Let *p*: "Light is scattered by molecules in the atmosphere" and *q*: "The sky is blue" then the converse *q* → *p*: "If the sky is blue then light is scattered by molecules in the atmosphere"
