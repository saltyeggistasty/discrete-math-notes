# **Contrapositive**

## Main Idea
Let *p* and *q* be propositions, then ¬*q* → ¬*p* is the contrapositive of the conditional statement *p* → *q*


My example:
  - Let *p*: "Light is scattered by molecules in the atmosphere" and *q*: "The sky is blue" then the contrapositive ¬*q* → ¬*p*: "If the sky is not blue then light isn't scattered by molecules in the atmosphere"
