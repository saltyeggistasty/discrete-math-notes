# **Biconditional Statement**

## Main Idea
The biconditional statement *p* ↔ *q* is true when both *p* and *q* has the same truth value, and is false otherwise.

| *p* | *q* | *p* ↔ *q* |
| - | - | ----- |
| T | T | T     |
| T | F | F     |
| F | T | F     |
| F | F | T     |


My example:
  - Let *p* be the statement "You have a driver license" and *q* be the statement "You can legally drive the car." Then *p* ↔ *q* is the statement "You can legally drive the car if and only if you have a driver license"
